## Summary

0.30.0 lets an ask that stops an agent's work reach the owner at once. Such an
ask is added as blocking (`asks add --blocking`), and in Autocode mode the
agent that asks raises one card for it in the owner's Inbox and records the
card on the row (`asks card <id> --inbox <card id>`). Every other ask waits
for the briefing as before (`rules/autocode.md` § 4, `contracts/autonomy.md`
§ 7).

A row of `_memory/asks.yaml` gains two optional fields: `blocking` (true or
false) and `inbox` (the card's id, or null). New rows carry both. A row
written before this version has neither and reads as a plain ask with no
card, so nothing in an existing queue needs converting. This step advances
`workspace/.version` and nothing else.
