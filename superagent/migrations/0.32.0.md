## Summary

0.32.0 makes the update loop simple. The check of the `update-light` duty is
due at every look, so every slot of its wake inside the update's work hours
runs a light update; it no longer skips a slot in which no watcher is due and
no document waits. And `watchlist check --due` takes a daily watcher at every
update run, where it took it once per calendar day. How often a daily source
is looked at is therefore how often an update runs. A weekly or a monthly
watcher keeps its period. A watcher's throttle
(`min_check_interval_minutes`) and its pack's call budget cap how often it is
fetched, as before.

Nothing in a workspace needs converting: no file changes shape. This step
advances `workspace/.version` and nothing else.

Two things to know before the step, because they change what runs:

- A workspace whose Updater runs as a session of its own now spends a model
  run on every slot of `update-light`. Fewer slots (the wake's trigger and
  `preferences.cadences.update_light`) or shorter work hours
  (`preferences.update.work_hours`) lower that.
- A daily source that should be looked at less often than an update runs
  needs a throttle on its ref (`min_check_interval_minutes`), or a weekly
  schedule. A daily `subagent` watcher costs a model run per look.
