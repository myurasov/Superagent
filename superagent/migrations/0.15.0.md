## Summary

0.15.0 lands the first supertailor-review's approved batch. Framework side:
credential redaction in the user-query log hook; collision-safe id minting
(`tools/next_id.py`); a read-only host memory-store detector
(`tools/memory_routing_check.py`); SimpleFin pending-to-posted reconciliation
(automatic per run, plus `--reconcile` one-shot repair); a project-drift
report (`tools/project_drift.py`) wired into `doctor`; a session-end
signal-capture sweep (`contracts/capture.md` § 7.2a) wired into the capture
skills and `doctor`/`supertailor-review`; alert archival-on-resolution with a
reordered `context.yaml` template and a new `alerts-archive.yaml` template;
"Waiting on your call" briefing blocks, world-graph rebuild cadence, and
discovery nudges in the cadence skills; tightened `refresh` triggers; two new
skills (`baton`, `email-refresh`); and the uv environment routed to
`./.venv.noSync/` with `UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT` carried by hooks and tool
shebangs (implements the approved st-2026-07-19-001).

Workspace-data leg is tiny: ensure `_memory/alerts-archive.yaml` exists so
the cadence skills' archival step has a destination. Alert pruning itself is
judgment work done by skills over time, not by this migration.
